# Engagement

One-paragraph purpose: what Umbrella Corporation is, why Loomery is engaged, and the
current phase — kept current here so any AI session gets it in one read.

## Summary

Umbrella Corporation is a multinational pharmaceutical and consumer health company
headquartered in Raccoon City, with an Applied Biosciences division running R&D labs
across six sites worldwide. Loomery is engaged to replace **UMBRA**, Applied
Biosciences' 20-year-old on-prem lab-operations system, with **Hive OS** — a modern,
compliance-grade platform covering facility access, sample inventory, and audit
reporting. The SOW was signed 2026-05-20; Discovery ran through July; delivery of Phase
1 (Facility Access & Sample Inventory) started 2026-08-06 and is now in Sprint 2.
(source: Granola note "SOW Sign-Off Call", 2026-05-20; Granola note "Phase 1 Scope
Review", 2026-07-30)

## Goals

Umbrella's "One Hive" initiative aims to consolidate six regional, independently-run
lab-ops systems onto a single platform before the 2027 FDA audit cycle — driven by
Priya Anand (Head of Compliance & Quality), who flagged UMBRA's lack of a unified audit
trail as the single biggest audit risk. Phase 1 success is measured on three things:
facility access requests resolved without a manual override, sample chain-of-custody
fully reconstructable from the system alone (zero paper backstops), and a successful
pilot cutover at the Raccoon City site with no UMBRA rollback. (source: Granola note
"Phase 1 Scope Review", 2026-07-30 — Priya: "if an auditor can't reconstruct
custody from the system alone, we've failed, full stop")

## Group interest

Hive OS is being watched above the Applied Biosciences division: at the quarterly
Group Steering Committee (2026-08-14), Group CIO Wendy Ashford flagged interest in
using it as the template for modernising Umbrella Pharmaceuticals' and Umbrella
Consumer Health's own legacy plant-ops systems, contingent on a clean Raccoon City
pilot cutover — meaning Phase 1's outcome now has weight beyond this one division.
Group CFO Robert Kaine confirmed Phase 1 budget is on track at the same meeting.
(source: Granola note "Group Steering Committee — Q3 Review", 2026-08-14)
