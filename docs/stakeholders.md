## Group-level (parent corporation)

Umbrella Corporation's Applied Biosciences division sits inside a larger group that
also runs Umbrella Pharmaceuticals and Umbrella Consumer Health; Hive OS is being
watched by the group as a template for the other two divisions' own ageing lab and
plant-ops systems, which is why group finance and the group CIO's office both showed
up at the steering committee rather than leaving this as a divisional project.

- **Robert Kaine** — Group CFO. Chairs the quarterly steering committee; holds the
  budget line Hive OS is funded from and the one who would have to approve extending
  it to the other two divisions. (source: Granola note "Group Steering Committee —
  Q3 Review", 2026-08-14)
- **Wendy Ashford** — Group CIO. Attends the steering committee as the sponsor
  evaluating whether Hive OS becomes the group-wide lab/plant-ops template; not
  involved in Phase 1 delivery decisions. (source: Granola note "Group Steering
  Committee — Q3 Review", 2026-08-14)
- **Trevor Lindqvist** — Lead auditor, Meridian & Cole (external audit firm engaged by
  Priya Anand's team ahead of the 2027 FDA cycle). Consulted on what Hive OS's audit
  export needs to prove; not a day-to-day contact. (source: Granola note "Audit
  Readiness Sync", 2026-08-11)
